I write novels about small coastal towns and the people who refuse to leave them. My second book, a story about a ferry captain and the island that keeps her, came out this spring with a small press that still sends handwritten notes. I live by the sea with a dog who is suspicious of the post.

Before writing full time I spent ten years as a sub-editor on a regional paper, which is where I learned that every sentence can lose three words. I still teach a weekend workshop on openings in the library above the harbour, and I answer every letter, eventually, usually by hand.
