Village library lends out its last typewriter

BySam Fakename
Northshire reporter
8 November 2026

A village library in Northshire has lent out its last typewriter, ending a scheme that let borrowers take one home for a fortnight.

Librarian Pat Example said the scheme began as a joke in 2019, when a resident donated three machines that had been sitting in a loft since the 1980s.

"People queued for them," she said. "Students wanted to write their personal statements without a phone buzzing beside them, and retired people wanted to write letters to grandchildren who had never received one."

Two of the machines eventually wore out, and the parish council said it could not fund repairs. The last one went to a borrower writing a memoir about her years on the ferries.

The library says it will keep the typewriter ribbons in stock for anyone who owns their own machine, and that a repair cafe has offered to look at the broken pair in the new year.
