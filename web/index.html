Hookr

The post is already in your book. Hookr finds it.

Open a page you wrote, like a Substack essay or a chapter, and Hookr pulls out the one line worth posting. One post for X, in your words, under 280 characters. Nothing invented.

Add Hookr to Chrome Free for authors. Firefox next.

So I gave myself a rule: the first draft is allowed to be terrible, and nobody is allowed to read it, including me.

Our promise

Hookr only quotes and trims. It never adds a thought you didn’t have, and it shows you exactly which sentence each post came from. If something can’t be traced back to your page, it doesn’t go out.

Publishers rarely market the backlist. Your best lines are sitting in it.

How it works

  1. Open something you wrote, in Chrome.
  2. Click the hook in your toolbar.
  3. Read the post, check where it came from, and copy it to X.

Install

Hookr is on its way to the Chrome Web Store. Until it lands, you can load it yourself in a minute:

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
